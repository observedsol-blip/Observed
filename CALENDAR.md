# Calendar (season 1)

Generation rules (frozen before season start):
- Feeds: SOL/USD, BTC/USD, ETH/USD, SKR/USD (only if Spike 1 confirms night-time coverage) — rotation defined below.
- A leaf holds a RULE, never a number: feed, offset in bps (e.g. +100 = "more than 1% above the reference"; may be negative), windows, confidence bound. The threshold is computed on-chain from the reference price = first Pyth update after 12:00 UTC (commit close). Offsets rotate from the fixed list [±50, ±100, ±150, ±200] bps; equality resolves No. Question text is generated by the client; always "above"/"below", never "at or above".
- Windows per round: commit 00:00-12:00 UTC, outcome 24:00 UTC, reveal until 12:00 UTC next day, resolve deadline outcome + 24 h.
- Season length: 64 leaves (Merkle depth 6); unused leaves = sha256(0x00 || [0;32]).
